# Carl AI financial planning requirements

Status: owner-requested scope expansion, September 29, 2026. Implementation and qualification pending.

Read this addendum together with [the preserved version 1.1 baseline](REQUIREMENTS.md). The owner clarified that a major part of Carl's job is to understand household debts, assets, cash flow, budgets and rental properties, and prepare plans to free cash, reduce debt, pay off credit cards, and assist with taxes and ownership structure. These are core application capabilities in the current build, not a future-only finance brief. This supersedes the baseline section 2 exclusion of debt-payoff optimization. It does not silently add credit applications, refinancing transactions, securities trading, a full accounting ledger, or external actions.

The owner confirmed the relevant location as Chester, Randolph County, Illinois, United States. Apply federal, Illinois and applicable local jurisdiction only after identifying the specific question, tax year and facts. Location does not establish filing status, residency history, entity classification, property ownership, assessed values, tax rates or eligibility. No actual financial balances or account access have been supplied.

## Architecture and operating boundary

Carl remains the single agent application. Its typed financial records, calculations, scenario engine, explanations, QQQ views and conversational capabilities share authorized domain services and PostgreSQL state. Extend the household model; do not attach a separate financial application or substitute conversation memory for records.

Recommendations, what-if plans, local reports and drafts are allowed. Carl cannot move money, pay creditors, trade, submit returns, apply for credit, transfer property, create legal entities, send vendor messages or make commitments. The subsequent Synology decision separately permits shared planning-calendar/reminder maintenance under [CALENDAR-REMINDERS.md](CALENDAR-REMINDERS.md). Human-initiated imports and local corrections remain narrow, attributed application operations. A proposed plan is never evidence of an executed payment or completed legal/tax action.

## Calculation contract

Use exact decimal amounts and explicit currencies, dated inputs, deterministic ordering and documented rounding. Distinguish stock balances from flows over a period. Connect property/account/debt references rather than summing overlapping representations of the same asset or liability. Joint ownership and partial access require explicit allocation/coverage, not assumed 100% ownership or a guessed household total.

Debt projections must declare interest accrual/payment timing, minimum-payment formulas, rate schedules, fees, new-charge assumptions, rounding and horizon. A simplified monthly model may support labeled estimates, but cannot be presented as an exact issuer payoff quote. Unspecified contractual terms remain unknown. Compare strategies against the same starting state and budget; respect minimum payments and reserves before allocating extra cash.

Financial goals, reserves, expense categories and scenario assumptions are human-editable records with provenance. They cannot override access rules. Tax rule packages need jurisdiction, tax year, effective/retrieved dates, primary source links and deterministic tests. No current tax rates, deductions, entity treatment or eligibility are asserted by this requirements document. Real tax guidance requires current source verification and sufficient case facts.

## Debt restructuring, execution support and household purchase decisions

The owner further clarified that Carl must compare debt solutions, support what-if planning and help carry selected plans through. Explicit examples are new loans, refinancing, moving debt to 0% or other loans, and snowball repayment. The owner also described the desired family iPhone interaction: while considering kitchen tables/chairs, ask what budget is available and whether to use cash, cards or store financing, and receive an actionable recommendation. These workflows are core current scope. New loans/refinancing/balance transfers are permitted scenario inputs; analysis does not itself authorize applications or money movement.

**FIN-14 — Complete debt strategies.** Compare the actual current-payment baseline, minimum-only baseline, avalanche, snowball, user-directed allocations, consolidation/new loans, refinancing and balance transfers, including explicitly defined combinations. Store offer terms/evidence, date and expiry, eligibility/approval status, amount/limit, upfront and financed fees, term, APR/rate changes, payment formula, collateral and applicable supplied prepayment conditions. Compare on consistent starting balances, available cash, reserve policy and horizon. Show total interest/fees/cost, monthly obligations, liquidity, payoff dates, break-even where meaningful and the cost of extending the term. Unknown offers/approvals/terms are labeled hypothetical; no invented eligibility or guaranteed savings. New financing proceeds and retiring old debts must reconcile as linked flows, not fresh income or duplicated liabilities. Distinguish true 0% promotions from deferred-interest contracts and test the actual supplied terms. If a contract's accrual/allocation rules are unsupported, identify the unsupported calculation rather than silently use a simple APR model.

**FIN-15 — Execute the selected plan as a tracked human workflow.** Persist versioned plan choice, goals, assumptions, assigned human steps, dependencies, requested documents, deadlines, user-recorded decisions and evidence of progress. Prepare application/document checklists, comparison questions and local drafts; help the owner compare an actual offer against the hypothetical plan. The owner clarified that execution is human-led: humans submit applications, sign agreements and perform transfers/payments outside Carl; no external submission/payment capability is introduced. Reconcile later Monarch records against expected plan effects, retain unexplained differences and distinguish user-asserted completion from independently observed outcomes. A proposed transfer or a payment-like transaction never proves a lender approved a loan or a debt is fully discharged. Revised plans require explicit selection, not silent replacement. No notification channel or cadence is implied.

**FIN-16 — Household purchase recommendation.** Through the family's supported conversational/iPhone API and Carl's QQQ records, answer whether a discretionary purchase is affordable, what all-in budget is supported and how to pay. Use permission-scoped current cash/balances, dated committed expenses, credible income, reserves, existing payment obligations, household goals, other selected plans and source freshness. Include taxes/delivery/fees when known and identify missing costs. Available credit, estimated home equity and unapproved loans are not a spending budget. Compare cash, an existing card paid in full, ordinary card borrowing and actual supplied store/other financing offers on total cost, timing, liquidity, repayment feasibility and impact on goals. Do not assume grace periods, rewards, eligibility or future income. Give a clear preferred option and supported limit (which may be zero or undetermined), explain material tradeoffs and list conditions that would change the answer. A lower payment alone does not establish affordability. Shared conversations must use an explicitly permitted shared scope; never reveal another member's private accounts. Incomplete access/data must limit the conclusion instead of producing a fabricated household-wide recommendation. Recommendations do not place orders or apply for credit.

### Source-informed financing semantics

The scenario engine must represent actual contract terms rather than treating every advertised promotion as equivalent. As checked September 29, 2026, the CFPB explains that a balance-transfer fee may apply to a zero-percent offer and that deferred-interest arrangements can impose accrued interest if their conditions are not met. These sources inform test cases; they do not establish the household's actual offer or eligibility:

- [CFPB: balance-transfer fees and zero-percent offers](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-balance-transfer-fee-can-a-balance-transfer-fee-be-charged-on-a-zero-percent-interest-rate-offer-en-53/)
- [CFPB: no interest if paid in full](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/i-got-a-credit-card-promising-no-interest-for-a-purchase-if-i-pay-in-full-within-12-months-how-does-this-work-en-40/)
- [CFPB: new purchases after a balance transfer](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/do-i-pay-interest-on-new-purchases-after-i-get-a-zero-or-low-rate-balance-transfer-en-49/)

The external financial-action boundary remains in force under the clarified human-led execution workflow. Calendar output is required; direct calendar-write versus downloadable-entry behavior remains a separate decision. Any later capability that submits applications, signs, purchases or moves money requires its own explicit scope, permitted integrations, authorization and qualification; it cannot enter through a scenario tool or source document.

## Interactive plans, expenses and affordability discipline

The owner clarified the intended interaction: discuss options with Carl, agree a plan, document who/what/when/where, receive reports/PDFs/calendar entries, execute human tasks, report progress or changed circumstances and revise or redo the plan together. This clarifies human-led financial execution. Carl's role includes maintaining durable plan state, not merely producing a one-time answer.

**FIN-17 — Collaborative plan lifecycle and deliverables.** Persist a plan's purpose, selected scenario, participants/audience, responsible people, actions, dates/deadlines, location/context, dependencies, supporting records, documents and success criteria. Distinguish proposed, selected, in progress, blocked, reported complete, evidenced complete, revised and superseded outcomes using a documented valid state model. Capture member check-ins and changes as attributed durable events; do not silently infer completion from conversation. Replan using current permitted evidence, explain what changed and retain previous versions and decisions. Explicit plan/check-in/update requests are narrow authenticated application operations with idempotency and optimistic concurrency; ordinary read tools do not hide these writes. A calendar event or user report alone is not proof of a payment, approval or debt discharge.

Provide readable local reports and downloadable PDFs of the selected plan, task assignments and progress, including plan version, as-of date, audience/scope, figures, sources and limitations. Recheck access on retrieval/export; generated files must not become unauthenticated public URLs. Keep imported text inert, prohibit arbitrary resource fetching during rendering and verify PDF layout with synthetic data. Produce usable calendar entries for the plan's dated tasks; these are authorized updates to the shared Synology calendar/reminders, governed by [CALENDAR-REMINDERS.md](CALENDAR-REMINDERS.md). Keep mutation authority restricted to the designated shared Synology planning workspace; other sources retain their granted scope. Explicitly distinguish planned deadlines from externally booked appointments and tasks from paid/completed actions.

**FIN-18 — Household and property expenses.** Model electricity/power, gas and other utilities, maintenance, repairs, insurance and other relevant expenses as actual transactions/bills, scheduled obligations and separately identified forecast assumptions. Assign permitted household/property/account/category relationships and explicit allocation when shared; never double-count a bill and its settlement or a reserve transfer and a repair expense. Retain frequency, due dates, variable/seasonal behavior, evidence of rate changes and source freshness when supplied. Support irregular maintenance/repair scenarios and configured reserves without pretending unknown future repairs are known liabilities. Compare budget/forecast to actual expense by period with coverage disclosed. Use these obligations in payoff and purchase decisions so cash that is needed for essential or upcoming costs is not presented as discretionary.

**FIN-19 — Credit is not an affordability budget.** Available credit is borrowing capacity, separate from available cash and sustainable purchase capacity. Carl must not recommend spending merely because a lender or card permits it. Give clear, respectful guidance that can recommend waiting, a smaller purchase or saving first when those better fit the selected goals. Classify an option as funded within the plan, dependent on additional borrowing, unsupported under the current plan or undetermined due to missing facts. Quantify the effect of new debt on interest/fees, monthly cash, payoff timing and reserves, and compare a no-purchase/delayed-purchase alternative. Lower minimum payments or refinanced terms are not automatically an improvement: show total cost, horizon, collateral and remaining capacity. A 0% offer is not free discretionary money; show fees, required repayment and expiry conditions. Avoid shame, sales pressure and blanket product recommendations. Use the owner's explicit priorities and assumptions; do not invent a reserve floor, spending ban or threshold. A user's choice does not rewrite the numerical evidence or silently change agreed goals; record explicit plan revisions.

The public implementation and examples encode these general product rules, not private family financial history or behavior. No real balances, account names or personal financial narrative are needed in public fixtures.

Develop investment education and scenario-based planning around the owner's confirmed time horizon, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, tax context, existing holdings and costs when supplied. Investment-stage readiness is agreed with the owner, not inferred from a generic debt-free threshold.
