# Tax and rental-structure preparation

Carl records human-supplied primary-source identities and applicability reviews. A URL, a supplied content hash, or a review note does not qualify a legal rule or establish an accountant's approval. Every packet remains a conditional preparation document with tax calculations `UNDETERMINED`; no entity recommendation, election, filing, title change or financial action is made.

The confirmed location is US / Illinois / Randolph County / Chester. The tax year, taxpayer/entity, ownership and basis remain unknown until supplied. A packet may retain an unknown tax year and explicitly reports the resulting applicability gap.

Use **Record Dated Primary Source** to record an official source's edition, actual retrieval time, applicable tax year, archive identity if available, and human applicability review evidence. No URL is fetched. A supplied hash does not prove that Carl has a retrievable archive; archive custody remains explicitly unverified. Review dates may extend at most one year, and an expired, future, deactivated, unarchived or wrong-year reference is not included as usable dated source evidence. Renew an edition/review by recording a new reference and a new alternative; old entries remain attributable. **Activate or Deactivate Tax Source** uses a reviewed record revision and invalidates older saved context when active status changes.

Use **Record Conditional Ownership Alternative** to describe a human-proposed arrangement for one accessible property, its supplied assumptions, selected source records, optional ownership evidence, and quoted setup/annual costs. Costs are supplied facts, not estimated savings. New alternatives preserve their source revisions; changing a linked source requires human re-review. Carl asks conditional questions about taxpayer treatment, title/elections, lender/insurer/legal review and missing costs without claiming a preferred structure.

Use **Prepare Tax and Ownership Discussion** to select properties, alternatives, references, a tax year if known and a nonfuture UTC as-of instant. The saved packet combines Carl's existing tax checklist with accessible supplied documents, reference-review limitations and conditional questions. Family workflow integration must pass the verified conversation audience to the same service; native QQQ generation is requester-only. Permission checks apply before collecting facts and again at persistence/retrieval. No private source becomes shared merely because an alternative was labeled family-visible.

A saved packet captures current supplied domain records. The requested as-of time evaluates source-reference applicability; it does not reconstruct earlier property, document or alternative state. Collection time is recorded separately. All source reviews include publication, retrieval, review and expiry dates so even unqualified references can be audited. Expiration is assessed against that snapshot's as-of date; it is not a claim that its references remain current forever. Property/source changes are shown as stale. Reference deactivation changes the permission epoch and denies old saved context. Generate a new packet to review current evidence.
