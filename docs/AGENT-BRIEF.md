# Carl AI brief

**Governing mission:** help the family get out of debt, improve rental-property and tax/ownership planning, then build an investment plan. Prioritize sustainable cash flow and reduction of expensive debt; evaluate every purchase, financing alternative and plan revision against the agreed goals. Investment timing depends on explicit household debt/liquidity/risk milestones, not a guessed rule that all debt must be zero.

Carl IS the application, including its household domain, database, reasoning, capabilities, workflows, and interfaces. The owner authorized a public standalone repository at KofTwentyTwo/carl, display name Carl AI, with the supplied version 1.1 draft as implementation baseline. Consumer identifiers are carl-ai / com.kof22.carlai. The reusable foundation remains a separately versioned dependency.

Required slices: authenticated domain access; manual bills and atomic CSV preview/import; grounded bill reads; Synology CalDAV synchronization and authorized shared-plan calendar/reminder maintenance, agendas and conflicts; vendor work and local drafts; permission-scoped on-demand household reports; real QQQ administration. Private conversations are default; family sharing must be explicit. The owner selected and authorized the existing personal1Password CLAUDE_API token for synthetic preview conversations; CLI access remains blocked. Permitted real data, real membership/access, household zone, budget/retention, and deployment remain unresolved. Development uses explicit synthetic members and clocks. Narrow authenticated internal persistence and the authorized Synology planning workspace are supported; financial/vendor external actions remain unavailable. See REQUIREMENTS.md and DECISIONS.md.

Financial planning is a core part of the current build: debts, assets, transaction-backed cash flow, budgets, credit-card payoff scenarios, rental-property economics, tax preparation support and ownership-structure analysis. [Financial requirements](FINANCIAL-PLANNING-REQUIREMENTS.md) supersede the original debt-optimization deferral while preserving human-led financial execution. Confirmed jurisdiction: Chester, Randolph County, Illinois, United States. Tax years, ownership facts and financial data remain unresolved; no personalized tax treatment is assumed.

Initial transaction source is manually downloaded Monarch CSV, with verified schema and required reconciliation in [Monarch import contract](MONARCH-IMPORT.md). Owner authorized local examination of supplied real examples only; public fixtures remain synthetic. Transaction exports alone do not establish current balances, debt terms or currency.

Core financial workflows also include consolidation/refinance/balance-transfer scenarios, tracked human execution of selected plans, and family purchase-budget/payment recommendations (FIN-14 through FIN-16). The kitchen table/chairs example is a required conversational acceptance case. External financial actions remain unavailable under the confirmed human-led execution workflow.

Carl is interactive throughout a plan lifecycle: agree a versioned approach, assign who/what/when/where, generate reports/PDFs/calendar entries, record human progress and replan. Household/property expenses including power, gas and maintenance feed forecasts. Borrowing capacity must remain distinct from affordability, with clear cost/goal impacts and the ability to recommend waiting or saving (FIN-17 through FIN-19). Financial execution is human-led; shared Synology calendar/reminder maintenance has standing owner authorization.

Current calendar authority: [Synology shared calendar/reminders](CALENDAR-REMINDERS.md) replaces iCloud and calendar-read-only assumptions. Carl may maintain plan entries under standing authority; human financial execution remains separate. Complete requirements include the preserved baseline, financial addendum, Monarch import contract and Synology contract; later confirmed decisions control their identified conflicts.
